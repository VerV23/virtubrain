Approach

How an engagement runs.

Four moves, in order. The same sequence works for a global close or a single payables team.

  1. 01

    Calibrate

    We lay your operating model against the VirtuBrain taxonomy for your industry and country, then agree the scope, down to the entity, the process and the steps in play.

    OutputA scoped process map, step by step

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    We walk the process with the people who run it and find the steps where work stalls. Every finding is tied to its step, with evidence from your own data.

    OutputFindings pinned to numbered steps

  3. 03

    Redesign

    For each step we decide who runs it, whether an agent alone, an agent that drafts for approval, or people. Then we design the controls and the evidence each step leaves.

    OutputA zone map, controls and an evidence standard

  4. 04

    Run

    We stand up the new model on the agreed slice and measure it against the KPIs set at the start. You decide what to scale from the results.

    OutputA working model and before-and-after measures

The three zones

Sort every step by howits work can be checked.

Autonomy follows verifiability. Where the output can be checked against a record, an agent runs it and logs the check. Where it can't, a person stays in the loop.

X-ray every step of the close

Auto

Output can be checked against a record. An agent runs it and the check is logged.

  • Bank reconciliationCheck: the statement ties to the ledger, item by item
  • Intercompany matchingCheck: both sides agree to the cent
  • Recurring journalsCheck: the entry matches the approved schedule

Assist

Output needs judgment to confirm. An agent drafts and a person approves.

  • AccrualsThe agent drafts from open POs, an accountant reviews
  • Flux analysisThe agent explains variances, the controller confirms
  • Balance sheet supportThe agent assembles, a reviewer signs

Principles

What we hold ourselves to.

Six rules that shape every engagement, from the first call to the handover.

  1. 01

    Name the step, not the theme.

    Every finding points to a numbered step your team can open and check.

  2. 02

    Automate what can be checked.

    An agent runs a step on its own only when its output can be checked against a record.

  3. 03

    Evidence before scale.

    Agent work grows only after the evidence from the first slice holds up in review.

  4. 04

    No promised percentages.

    We don't quote improvement figures until we have measured your process with your data.

  5. 05

    People stay accountable.

    Agents prepare and execute. Sign-off stays with a named person.

  6. 06

    One process first.

    A bounded pilot that works beats a program that is still being planned.
