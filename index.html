8.1.1 · Finance & Accounting

Execute Period-End and Continuous Close

Starts when Triggered by an approved mandate to execute period-end and continuous close

Industry lens · Retail

High-volume, multi-location retailers must reconcile thousands of daily point-of-sale settlement batches, gift card/loyalty liabilities, and inventory-in-transit accruals before the books can close, making automated POS-to-GL matching and store-level cutoff discipline critical to a fast close.

Country note · Philippines

Philippine entities must align close timing with BIR's monthly/quarterly VAT (2550M/2550Q) and withholding tax (1601-EQ) filing deadlines, and journal entries affecting revenue must reconcile to BIR-accredited Computerized Accounting System (CAS) records to avoid discrepancies at audit.

Typical systems

  • BlackLine
  • SAP S/4HANA
  • Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP
  • Workiva

KPIs

  • Days to close (calendar days)
  • % journal entries auto-matched/touchless
  • Number of late/manual adjusting entries post-close

Three zones

Sort every step by who should run it.

One question decides it. How can the output be checked?

  • AutoOutput can be checked against a record. An agent runs it and the check is logged.
  • AssistOutput needs judgment to confirm. An agent drafts and a person approves.
  • HumanAccountability can't be delegated. People decide and agents prepare.
