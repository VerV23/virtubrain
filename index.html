Three zones

Sort each step by how its output can be checked.

Against a record, with judgment, or by someone who must answer for it.

  • AutoOutput can be checked against a record. An agent runs it and the check is logged.
  • AssistOutput needs judgment to confirm. An agent drafts and a person approves.
  • HumanAccountability can't be delegated. People decide and agents prepare.
