What stays human

A person stays in charge.

You decide where the assistant stops. We set these handover rules with you before it goes live.

  1. IfA customer asks for a refund or an exception to your policy

    ThenIt gathers the facts and hands over to your staff.

  2. IfSomeone is upset

    ThenIt passes the conversation to a person straight away.

  3. IfA question touches legal, health or safety matters

    ThenA person answers it, every time.

  4. IfA topic is on the list you keep for staff only

    ThenIt doesn't answer on its own and hands over.
